🧠 Why we can't seem to decide

It's a well-known phenomenon: the paradox of choice. The more options we have, the harder it gets to decide — and the less satisfied we feel.

Streaming catalogs put thousands of titles in front of us… and our brain overloads. The result: we procrastinate, we scroll, we give up.

“The fix is counterintuitive: deliberately cut down your options instead of forever exploring more.”

🎯 The 4-step method

1
Pick a mood, not a filmLaugh, thrill, emotion, action? A single mood already cuts 90% of the catalog.
2
Set a runtimeEarly night? Aim under 1h45. Runtime rules out a ton of candidates fast.
3
Cap it at 3 optionsThree films, not thirty. Your brain decides in just a few seconds.
4
Decide and commitThe best film is the one you actually press play on. Choose it and enjoy.
